# AI’ll Remember

## Project Overview

**AI’ll Remember** is an AI-powered, gamified knowledge-retention application designed to help students retain what they learn over time. The system tracks student performance, monitors topic mastery, identifies potential knowledge decay, and recommends personalized review activities to reinforce learning.

By combining AI-assisted recommendations with game-inspired features, the application aims to make reviewing lessons more engaging, motivating, and effective.

## Problem Statement

Students may understand a topic during a lesson or perform well on an examination but gradually forget the information over time. Traditional grading systems primarily measure performance at a particular point rather than continuously monitoring long-term knowledge retention.

Reviewing can also become repetitive and inefficient when students spend time on topics they already understand while neglecting topics they struggle to remember.

AI’ll Remember aims to address these challenges by tracking learning performance, identifying topics that may need review, and recommending suitable review activities.

## Objectives

The project aims to:

- Monitor student performance and topic mastery over time.
- Identify topics that may be experiencing knowledge decay.
- Provide AI-assisted, personalized review recommendations.
- Encourage consistent learning through gamification.
- Help students focus their review efforts on topics that need additional practice.

## Key Features

### 1. Knowledge and Mastery Tracking
Tracks quiz performance and topic mastery to help students understand their learning progress.

### 2. Knowledge Decay Detection
Identifies topics that may require additional review based on student performance and learning history.

### 3. AI-Powered Review Recommendations
Provides personalized review suggestions based on topics that need reinforcement.

### 4. Review Quests
Presents recommended review activities as quests that students can complete to reinforce their knowledge.

### 5. Gamification
Uses experience points (XP), levels, and streaks to encourage continued participation and consistent review habits.

### 6. Progress Dashboard
Displays learning progress and topic mastery to help students identify strengths and areas for improvement.

## System Workflow

The proposed system follows this general workflow:

1. The student accesses the application and selects a subject or topic.
2. The student completes a quiz or review activity.
3. The system records the results and updates topic mastery.
4. The system analyzes performance and identifies topics that may need review.
5. AI-assisted recommendations suggest suitable review activities.
6. The student completes a recommended Review Quest.
7. The system updates progress and awards applicable rewards.
